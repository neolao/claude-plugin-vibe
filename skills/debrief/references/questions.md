# Retrospective Questions

## Purpose
Example wordings for the closing questions. Pick from the real context of the task rather than asking the three families mechanically.

## Hesitations during the task
- "When did you hesitate, or were unsure about the approach I was taking?"
- "Is there a choice I made without consulting you that you would have decided differently?"

## Validated choices
- "Choice [X] suited you — should I repeat it in a similar context next time?"

## Open
- "Anything else to change in how I work on this kind of task?"

## What not to do
Do not ask the three families systematically. A task with no ambiguity and no correction deserves no question about hesitations — the debrief must stay rare and targeted, or it becomes noise the user ignores.
