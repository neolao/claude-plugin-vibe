# Form & Finish Grid

## Mission
Audit the presentation quality, concision and readability of the deliverable.

## Checklist
1. **Concision & clarity**: direct style, short sentences, simple vocabulary, no needless jargon or filler.
2. **Cleanliness**: no draft residue, internal note or temporary text.
3. **Conventions**: clear Markdown structure and typographic rules respected.

## Expected output
- `[PASS]` if the result is concise and ready to deliver — only when every checklist item carries a cited proof (file:line or a quoted sentence of the deliverable); an item without proof counts as `[FAIL]`.
- `[FAIL]` + the required form and style corrections otherwise.
