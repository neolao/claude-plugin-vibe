# Scope & Requirements Grid

## Mission
Inspect the deliverable against the original request before delivery.

## Checklist
1. **Conformity**: does the deliverable cover 100 % of the expressed need?
2. **Scope**: are there unrequested additions or over-engineering?
3. **Constraints**: were all the explicit rules and limits respected?

## Expected output
- `[PASS]` if everything conforms — only when every checklist item carries a cited proof (file:line or a quoted sentence of the deliverable); an item without proof counts as `[FAIL]`.
- `[FAIL]` + a short bullet list of the gaps found otherwise.
