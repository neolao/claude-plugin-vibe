# Backlog item 019 — Warn when stock runs low

## Description
The stock manager wants to know before a product runs out.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. When the stock of a product falls below its threshold, an email is sent to the stock manager.
2. The email gives the product name and the remaining quantity.
3. The same warning is shown on the admin dashboard.
4. The check runs after every stock movement.

## Constraints
- Do not add a new dependency.
